# Ocean ambience

- Recording: **Ocean waves, Kalundborg Fjord** / **Oceanwavescrushing.ogg**
- Author: **Luftrum**
- Original source: https://freesound.org/people/Luftrum/sounds/48412/
- Download source and license evidence: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Oceanwavescrushing.ogg
- License: **Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 Unported** — https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/

`ocean-loop.wav` is an adaptation: low-frequency rumble reduction, gentle high-frequency filtering, resampling to 22,050 Hz stereo, rearrangement, level adjustment, and six-second equal-power crossfades to make a 138-second seamless loop. No synthesizer, generated white noise, or tonal drone is added. The original author does not endorse this project.
