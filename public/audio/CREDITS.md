# Ocean ambience

- Recording: **Ocean waves, Kalundborg Fjord** / **Oceanwavescrushing.ogg**
- Author: **Luftrum**
- Original source: https://freesound.org/people/Luftrum/sounds/48412/
- Download source and license evidence: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Oceanwavescrushing.ogg

`ocean-loop.wav` is an adaptation: low-frequency rumble reduction, gentle high-frequency filtering, resampling to 22,050 Hz stereo, rearrangement, level adjustment, and six-second equal-power crossfades. The waves are slowed to 64.6875% speed, trimmed and crossfaded into a 160-second seamless loop. The wave layer uses gentle 110 Hz high-pass and 2.1 kHz low-pass filtering, smoothed energy-based attenuation of stronger surges, and an additional 20% level reduction from the previous 0.3-gain mix to soften crashing waves and spray. Waves rest twice, at 48–54 seconds and 108–114 seconds, each with a ten-second fade-out before and ten-second fade-in after; music continues throughout. The original sparse piano-style arpeggio and melody retain their tempo and gain. Musical note tails and quiet echoes wrap across the loop boundary. No generated noise or sustained bass drone is added. The original recording author does not endorse this project. The added music is an original procedural composition created for Scout.
